## 🧭 What Is NoEyes?

**NoEyes** is an experimental sensory-substitution training environment that helps curious learners develop an alternative form of spatial perception using only audio cues, vibration patterns, and structured mental mapping exercises — all while wearing a soft opaque blindfold. The project grew out of a simple, almost playful question: *what if a person could learn to "see" a room the way a bat navigates a cave?* Instead of bouncing sound waves off cave walls, our learners bounce carefully designed audio pulses off their own attention.

This repository contains the complete training framework: a progressive curriculum, a lightweight cross-platform runtime, a multi-language coaching interface, and a rich set of practice scenarios that gradually build up a personal "acoustic vocabulary" for the space around you. The goal is not to replace vision, medical treatment, or professional mobility training. The goal is to celebrate human adaptability, sharpen spatial memory, and give hobbyists, researchers, and accessibility enthusiasts a respectful, well-documented playground for exploring echolocation-inspired perception.

Think of NoEyes as a **gym for your spatial imagination**. Every session is a small expedition into a room you thought you already knew. Over time, the room starts answering back.

## 🎯 Project Vision

Most sensory training tools treat the learner as a passive recipient: an app beeps, a user reacts. NoEyes flips that relationship. The learner is the explorer. The software is the terrain. The blindfold is not a barrier but a doorway. We believe that perception is partly a learned skill, and that with patience, structured practice, and warm encouragement, anyone can expand the richness of how they experience physical space.

This is a labor of love, curiosity, and respect for the human nervous system's astonishing plasticity.

## 🏗️ How It Works (Conceptually)

Imagine standing in the middle of a quiet room. You are wearing the blindfold. A soft tone plays from a small device resting in your palm. As you rotate slowly, the tone's character changes — brighter when you face an open doorway, muffled when a bookshelf blocks the path. Your brain, given enough repetitions and enough patience, begins to stitch these changing tones into a mental picture.

NoEyes guides that stitching process through four stages:

1. **Anchor Stage** — Learning a single fixed reference tone associated with one known object.
2. **Contrast Stage** — Comparing two or three objects, distinguishing them purely by acoustic signature.
3. **Sweep Stage** — Rotating or walking slowly to build a continuous sense of direction.
4. **Reconstruction Stage** — Mentally assembling an entire room layout from a sequence of sweeps.

Each stage has dozens of micro-exercises, and the coach voice celebrates every small victory with genuine warmth.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Three principles guide every decision in this project:

1. **Respect the Learner.** No gamified pressure, no streak-shaming, no artificial urgency. Progress is honored at whatever pace feels right.
2. **Respect the Body.** Sessions are short by design. The engine actively discourages marathon training. Rest is treated as part of the curriculum, not as failure.
3. **Respect the Truth.** NoEyes is not a medical device and does not claim to restore vision. It is a perception-training curiosity, and we say so plainly.

We also believe that software should feel *warm*. The coach voice uses encouraging language, the interface avoids harsh red error states, and even a missed exercise produces a gentle "let's try that sweep again."
